Term 23 (2007–2011) — Publication Brief
========================================================================

Core claim: Term 23 is strongest when read as a reframing sequence: the closure case lowers AKP's constitutional negativity, the global crisis pushes both AKP and CHP into macroeconomic attack, and the 2010 referendum restores AKP's democratic-reform legitimation.

Target outlets: Political Communication, Comparative Political Studies, BJPS (stretch)

Strongest event findings:
- AKP constitutional negativity collapses after the closure case: down (RR=0.25, p=4.23e-05, 0.205->0.051; event=2008-07).
- AKP also shifts into macroeconomic negativity after the crisis: up (RR=2.30, p=2.68e-13, 0.153->0.350; event=2008-09).
- CHP macroeconomic negativity surges with the global crisis: up (RR=1.78, p=3.13e-14, 0.304->0.540; event=2008-09).
- AKP democratic-reform legitimation rises after the closure case: up (RR=1.44, p=1.78e-05, 0.336->0.483; event=2008-07).
- AKP referendum campaign leans on democratic-reform positivity: up (RR=1.19, p=0.0024, 0.388->0.461; event=2010-09).
