Term 25 (2015 Interregnum) — Publication Brief
========================================================================

Core claim: Term 25 is too short for standard long-window inference, but it yields a sharp interregnum note: July and September are security-compressed months, whereas October re-centers AKP's agenda on macroeconomy before the Nov. 1 election while reformist positivity collapses.

Target outlets: Electoral Studies (research note), Turkish Studies, Party Politics (short-format angle)

Strongest event findings:
- AKP sharply recenters macroeconomy before the Nov. 1 election: up (RR=2.68, p=2.21e-05, 0.130->0.349; event=2015-10).
- AKP's democratic-reform positivity also drops before the election: down (RR=0.45, p=0.0303, 0.780->0.348; event=2015-10).
- AKP's macroeconomic positivity falls as it recenters the agenda: down (RR=0.46, p=0.00123, 1.444->0.658; event=2015-10).
