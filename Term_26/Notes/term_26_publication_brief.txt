Term 26 (2015–2018) — Publication Brief
========================================================================

Core claim: Term 26 is strongest as an authoritarian consolidation sequence. The coup and OHAL compress pluralism, intensify HDP's macroeconomic distress language, turn AKP's democratic-reform talk more negative, and then constitutionalize conflict during the referendum campaign.

Target outlets: Democratization, Journal of Democracy, Comparative Political Studies

Strongest event findings:
- AKP constitutional negativity falls after HDP arrests as repression normalizes: down (RR=0.52, p=2.74e-11, 0.527->0.274; event=2016-11).
- HDP macroeconomic negativity spikes after the coup attempt: up (RR=1.92, p=1.08e-12, 0.351->0.675; event=2016-07).
- CHP referendum campaign invests in constitutional legitimation: up (RR=1.59, p=0.000309, 0.132->0.210; event=2017-04).
- AKP's democratic-reform rhetoric turns markedly more negative after the coup: up (RR=1.56, p=7.09e-08, 0.187->0.291; event=2016-07).
- HDP's macroeconomic agenda expands sharply under OHAL shock: up (RR=1.50, p=1.26e-10, 0.116->0.173; event=2016-07).
