Term 27 (2018–2023) — Publication Brief
========================================================================

Core claim: Term 27 is most convincing as a monetary narrative war under presidential atrophy. Parliament's overall weight falls, yet Albayrak's resignation reactivates constitutional attack and the 2022 FX crisis reallocates opposition speech toward macroeconomic attack.

Target outlets: Party Politics, Comparative Politics, Political Economy journal outlets

Strongest event findings:
- CHP constitutional negativity doubles after the Albayrak break: up (RR=2.22, p=0, 0.217->0.481; event=2020-11).
- AKP itself also shifts toward constitutional conflict after Albayrak: up (RR=1.71, p=7.24e-06, 0.288->0.493; event=2020-11).
- AKP security negativity softens as the agenda shifts back to the economy: down (RR=0.62, p=5.43e-05, 0.498->0.311; event=2022-01).
- IYI Party dramatically expands its macroeconomic agenda in the FX crisis: up (RR=1.47, p=0, 0.490->0.719; event=2022-01).
- CHP macroeconomic negativity intensifies in the FX crisis: up (RR=1.19, p=0.00101, 0.426->0.508; event=2022-01).
