Term 28 (2023–2026) — Publication Brief
========================================================================

Core claim: Term 28 is strongest as a judicial-backlash period. Local-election losses destabilize the ruling coalition, the İmamoğlu case pushes AKP into constitutional conflict, and the opposition splits into distinct democratic-reform and constitutional lanes rather than a single undifferentiated bloc.

Target outlets: Electoral Studies, Journal of Democracy, Comparative Political Studies

Strongest event findings:
- MHP security negativity surges after the local-election shock: up (RR=1.88, p=3.9e-06, 0.363->0.682; event=2024-03).
- IYI Party shifts into constitutional conflict after the İmamoğlu shock: up (RR=1.64, p=1.85e-11, 0.090->0.148; event=2025-03).
- AKP constitutional negativity rises after the İmamoğlu case: up (RR=1.49, p=0.00177, 0.311->0.465; event=2025-03).
- AKP macroeconomic positivity retreats after the protest escalation: down (RR=0.76, p=1.21e-05, 0.873->0.665; event=2025-04).
- CHP macroeconomic attack intensifies during the protest month: up (RR=1.29, p=4.83e-06, 0.393->0.507; event=2025-04).
- CHP expands its democratic-reform agenda after the judicial shock: up (RR=1.16, p=8.74e-07, 0.399->0.464; event=2025-03).
